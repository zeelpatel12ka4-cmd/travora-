Our Story

We're on a Mission to Make Travel Effortless

Travora was born from a simple frustration: planning a great trip takes too many hours, too many tabs, and too much guesswork. We're fixing that with AI.

Our Mission

Turn travel planning from a chore into a joy

We believe the best travel experiences start with a great plan. But planning — researching destinations, building itineraries, tracking budgets, finding hidden gems — takes days of effort that most people don't have.

Travora changes that. Our multi-agent AI handles all of it, producing a personalised, ready-to-use itinerary in seconds — so you can spend your energy on what actually matters: experiencing the world.

The Technology

Meet the AI Agents Behind Travora

Every trip is planned by a team of four specialised AI agents working in concert.

Research Agent

Gathers destination intelligence: weather for your travel dates, visa requirements, safety notes, local transport, and the best neighbourhoods to base yourself in.

Itinerary Builder

Creates a detailed, realistic day-by-day plan — balancing must-see highlights with local experiences, factoring in travel time, opening hours, and your pace preference.

Budget Optimizer

Allocates your budget intelligently across flights, accommodation, food, and activities. Flags overspends and suggests money-saving alternatives without sacrificing experience.

Local Experience Agent

The insider guide. Surfaces hidden gems, must-eat restaurants, cultural etiquette tips, and local events that most tourists never discover from standard travel sites.
